We want to add more AI features after the MVP.

- **Smart Summaries**: Automatically summarize long email threads.
- **Tone Adjustment**: Let users choose between "Professional", "Friendly", and "Direct" for email drafts.
- **Meeting extraction**: Auto-generate tasks from [[2024-01-15-Sprint-Planning]] and similar notes.
